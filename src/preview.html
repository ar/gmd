Gmail opens with a plain-text version of your message. To use the formatting shown below:

  1. Copy the formatted email.
  2. Open Gmail, click inside the message body, and select the body text.
  3. Paste with Ctrl+V or ⌘V to replace it with the formatted message. Add attachments and review.
2. Open Gmail

Copying does not fill Gmail automatically. Keep Gmail’s “Plain text mode” off and use normal paste.
